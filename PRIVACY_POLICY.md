**What the app does:** it lets a school or teacher keep student, attendance, fee and exam-result records on their own phone.

**Data you enter:** student names, parent names, class, roll number, phone numbers, fee amounts, attendance and marks. This data is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.

**We do not collect your data.** The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We (the developer) never receive, see or share the records you enter. Nothing is sent over the internet by the app.

**Backup:** the Backup feature shows your data as text. If you copy it and save or send it somewhere, that is your choice and under your control.

**Your control and deletion:** you can edit or delete any record inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all data.

**Children:** the app is for adults (teachers and school staff). It contains information about students, which you are responsible for handling lawfully, for example with parents' or the school's authorisation.

**Security:** data stays in the app's private storage and device backup to Google is disabled for this app. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address.
